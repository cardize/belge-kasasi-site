Frequently asked questions

The deadline wasn't read, or was read wrong

Photograph the document on a flat surface, in good light, with the date clearly visible. The date it reads is always yours to confirm; if it's wrong, pick it yourself in the form.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on in Settings › Notifications › DocVault. Reminders arrive at 9:00 AM on the days you chose.

My lifetime purchase doesn't show

Tap "Restore purchase" in the app's Settings. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you used for the purchase.

What happens to my documents if I change phones?

Documents are kept only on your device. If you move to a new phone from an iCloud backup or with direct device-to-device transfer, your documents come along. The app has no cloud account of its own.

Can anyone see my documents?

No. The app sends nothing off your device. You can also turn on the app lock in Settings to require Face ID or your device passcode.
