Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short: DocVault collects no personal data. Your documents, photos and notes are stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

Who we are

DocVault (Belge Kasası in Turkish) is a mobile app developed by Mustafa Sami Sakarya. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data we collect

None. The app sends no data to us or to any other company. No usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or location are collected, and there is no tracking across apps.

What is stored on your device

For each document you add, the following is kept only in the app's own storage on your device:

  • The document's name, type, deadline, your notes and reminder days
  • The photo of the document you took or chose
  • Settings such as the app lock and your purchase status

We cannot access any of this.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photos are accessed only when you add a document, and only with your permission. The app saves a copy of the photo you choose in its own storage; it doesn't read the other photos in your library. Reading the deadline uses your device's built-in text recognition (Apple Vision on iOS) and runs entirely on the device; the photo is never uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No data is sent to a server or a notification service for them.

Face ID and device passcode

If you turn on the app lock, iOS performs the check. The app never sees your face data, fingerprint or passcode; it only learns whether the check succeeded.

Purchases

Lifetime access is sold through the App Store and Apple processes the payment. Your payment details never reach us; the app only learns whether the purchase was made. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is on, your app data may be included in that backup. The backup is kept in your Apple account under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document also deletes its photo and reminders. Removing the app from your device deletes all of its data; any copy in an iCloud backup is managed from your Apple account settings.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, so it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with an updated effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so clearly on this page beforehand.
